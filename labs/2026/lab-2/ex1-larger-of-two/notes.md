\# Exercise 1: Larger of Two Integers



\## Purpose



The program compares two integers and prints the larger integer.



\## Register Usage



\* `t0`: Stores the address of a variable.

\* `t1`: Stores the first integer.

\* `t2`: Stores the second integer.

\* `a0`: Holds the integer to print.

\* `a7`: Holds the system call number.



\## Challenges



Understanding conditional branching and selecting the larger integer using `bge` was the main challenge.
